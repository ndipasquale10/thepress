Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

The short version. If you never sign in, nothing you enter leaves your phone. Signing in with Google syncs your rounds so you can reach them from another device. We show no ads, run no analytics, and sell nothing to anybody.

1. Who this covers

This policy describes how The Press (“we”, “the app”) handles information when you use the app, whether from a browser or installed to your home screen.

2. What we collect, and when

If you never sign in

Nothing is collected and nothing is transmitted. Player names, handicaps, scores, courses, stakes and results are stored only in your device’s local storage. We cannot see any of it. Clearing your browser data erases it, and it is not recoverable.

If you sign in with Google

Signing in is optional and unlocks syncing across devices, live rounds, and the shared course library. Through Google Sign-In we receive:

  • Your Google account identifier, name, email address, and profile photo URL.

We never receive your Google password. Once signed in, the following is copied from your device to our database so it survives a lost phone:

  • Player profiles you have saved — names and handicaps, including those of people you play with.
  • Courses you have saved or entered by hand.
  • Round history — scores, the game and stakes played, and the resulting amounts won or owed.

3. Live rounds are readable by anyone with the code

When you tap Go Live, that round — the course, every player’s name, the running scores and the running money — is stored on our server under a six-character share code.

  • Anyone who has that code can read the round. Anyone who has the code and is signed in can also enter scores into it.
  • A watch link (?watch=CODE) can be opened by someone who is not signed in at all.
  • The code is the only protection. Treat it like the link to a shared document: give it to your group, not to a public channel.
  • Nobody can search or browse for rounds. A round can only be reached by its exact code, and only you can list the rounds you host.

Live rounds persist until you delete them or delete your account.

4. The shared course library

Courses you save are also published to a library available to every signed-in player, so the next person who plays your course does not have to type in the card. This contains course data only — name, city, state, tee ratings, slopes, yardages, pars and stroke indexes. It contains no player names, scores, or personal information, and it is not attributed to you. Because other players come to depend on it, library entries are not removed when you delete your account.

5. Payments

The Press never accepts, holds, or transfers money. The settle-up buttons open Venmo, Cash App, or PayPal with the amount and note pre-filled; the payment happens entirely in that app, between you and the other player. We do not receive confirmation that you paid, and we send those services nothing beyond what is contained in the link you tap. If you save a Venmo handle to a player profile, it is stored with that profile exactly like a name.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your information.
  • We do not show advertising and we do not share data with advertisers.
  • We run no analytics or tracking SDKs, and set no advertising cookies.
  • We do not use your golf data to build a profile of you for any purpose other than showing it back to you.

7. Who processes data on our behalf

We use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) for sign-in and storage, and static web hosting to serve the app. Google processes this data as our service provider under its own terms. No other third party receives it.

8. Notifications

If you allow notifications, they are generated locally by your browser to tell you about your own round. We do not operate a push service and send you no messages.

9. How long we keep it

Synced data is kept until you delete it. Deleting a round in the app removes it from your device and from your synced copy. Deleting your account removes everything, as described below.

10. Your choices and rights

  • Use the app without an account. Every scoring and betting feature works signed out. Only syncing, live rounds, and the shared library require sign-in.
  • Export your data. Settings → Export Data downloads everything the app holds about you as a JSON file you can keep or move.
  • Delete your data. Settings → Delete Account & Data erases your synced record, the live rounds you host, your Google sign-in link, and everything stored on the device. It is immediate and permanent, and the app will tell you if any part of it fails rather than reporting a deletion that did not happen. Shared course-library entries, which contain no personal information, remain.
  • Access and correction. Every field the app holds is visible and editable in the app itself.

If you are in the EEA, the UK, or California, the rights your law gives you — access, correction, deletion, portability, and objection — are served by the export and delete controls above. You may also contact us directly.

11. Children

The Press is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Security

Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS). Access to stored data is enforced by server-side security rules that are version-controlled, tested automatically on every change, and deployed from source. No system is perfectly secure, and a live-round share code protects a round only as well as you protect the code.

13. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top and show a notice in the app.
